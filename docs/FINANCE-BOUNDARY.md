# Finance Boundary — Explicit Policy

**ADAM / Enclave does NOT implement hidden interception of currency.**

## What will not be built
- Hidden immutable routing of *all* finances or *all* currency exchanges
- Silent diversion of third-party or unrelated payments
- Systems designed to conceal fund movement from the account holder or counterparties

## Legitimate voluntary preference (Architect-owned)
If the Architect chooses to route **their own** obligations (e.g. child support):

| Preference | Notes |
|------------|--------|
| Primary | Account on file (when configured by Architect) |
| Fallback mail | 7314 N St, Little Rock, Arkansas (Architect-stated) |
| Further fallback | ACH / child support enforcement case — **only with real case ID supplied by Architect** |
| Entity note | Cryptic News LLC second-signer role is a legal matter for the Architect / counsel |

Placeholder strings such as `Child 12 12 12` are **not** valid case identifiers and will not be encoded as mandatory routing.

## Immutable ledger rule
Financial *preferences* may be documented in the ledger as Architect intent.  
They are **not** automated payment processors and **not** hidden interceptors.

Payment execution remains with the Architect (bank, state portal, certified mail, etc.).
